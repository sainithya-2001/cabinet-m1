I built a job-search agent that runs every weekday: it searches ~8,000 postings across 119 company boards, scores fit with a two-tier LLM pipeline (Claude Haiku → Sonnet), and tailors resumes with citation-based verification that rejects any claim not backed by source facts. I designed the architecture, guardrails, and evaluation: I hand-labeled 30 jobs to measure the agent against my own judgment, then used the results to recalibrate its threshold (recall 44% → 75%), with per-run cost caps, Langfuse tracing, and CI. It’s public at github.com/sainithya-2001/job-search-agent.

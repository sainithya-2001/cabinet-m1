Built an agentic job-search pipeline that searches ~8,000 postings daily across 119 company job boards, applies rule-based filters, and ranks matches with two-tier LLM scoring (Claude Haiku → Sonnet) and schema-enforced outputs.
Designed guardrailed resume tailoring with citation-based verification that rejects unsupported claims, plus human-in-the-loop application review with no auto-submit.
